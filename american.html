Our Schools

American School

Inquiry-based learning. Lifelong learners.

Overview

A dynamic, inquiry-based education

Our American School provides a dynamic, inquiry-based education that encourages students to think critically, collaborate effectively and become lifelong learners.

Learning is student-centered, standards-based and designed to develop confident communicators who are ready for college and career pathways.

Students benefit from

  • Standards-based curriculum
  • Project-based learning
  • Continuous assessment
  • Technology integration
  • Leadership development
  • College & career readiness
Teaching & learning

How students learn

Inspire

Cultivate curiosity, enthusiasm and a genuine passion for learning.

Engage

Create active, meaningful and collaborative learning experiences.

Support

Provide equitable opportunities and a nurturing environment where every learner can succeed.

Reflect

Help students evaluate their learning, celebrate success and identify next steps.

Empower

Develop confident, independent learners who take ownership of their education.

Assess for growth

Use diagnostic, formative and summative assessment to inform teaching and student progress.
