Our Story

In honour of our founder

Hajj Mohamed Abdel Moneim Abou Nar was the patriarch of the Abou Nar family and the founder and owner of Al Manara Schools in Damanhour, Beheira Governorate. He served as Director of Private Education and was the Representative of Private School Owners across Beheira Governorate from 1996 to 2016, reflecting his longstanding contribution to the development of private education in the governorate.

He passed away in January 2017, leaving behind a distinguished educational legacy built over decades of leadership, dedication, and commitment to education. One of his greatest dreams was to establish an international school that would provide future generations with a high-quality, internationally recognised education while remaining deeply rooted in the values and principles on which Al Manara was founded.

Today, his children and grandchildren are proudly carrying forward his vision and completing the journey he began. The establishment and development of Al Manara International School represents the continuation and fulfilment of his dream, alongside the continued growth of Al Manara’s Language and Arabic divisions.

His vision became their mission, and his legacy continues through every new generation of Al Manara students.

“A lighthouse does not shine for itself; it shines so that others may find their way.”

His light continues to guide us
The name El Manara

The Lighthouse

The story of El Manara began with a simple yet powerful belief: education has the power to transform lives, strengthen communities, and shape a brighter future. In the early 1990s, two distinguished educational institutions were founded under the name El Manara — a National Arabic School and a National Language School.

The name El Manara, meaning “The Lighthouse,” reflects our purpose and our promise. A lighthouse stands as a symbol of guidance, hope, resilience and vision. It illuminates the way forward, offering direction through uncertainty and inspiring confidence in those who seek their path.

Every student who enters our school becomes part of a legacy that began more than three decades ago: a legacy of learning, leadership, integrity and hope.
