Welcome to El Manara

A legacy of education, a vision for the future

Dear Parents, welcome to El Manara International School, where every child embarks on a journey of discovery, growth and excellence. Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes.

Through a strong partnership between our dedicated educators and our families, we cultivate academic excellence, integrity, creativity and leadership while preparing students to thrive in an ever-changing global society.

Whether your child joins our American or British Division, they will experience an education that nurtures not only the mind but also the character.

Our Story & Founder
The Heart Behind El Manara

In Loving Memory

Late Mr. Mohamed Bahaa-ElDin Abounar Founder of El Manara Schools

A great educator’s legacy is not measured by the buildings he constructed, but by the generations of lives he inspired.

With unwavering vision, dedication, and a profound belief in the transformative power of education, the late Mr. Mohamed Bahaa-ElDin Abounar established the foundations upon which El Manara Schools have proudly stood for more than three decades.

His dream was to create schools where knowledge would be nurtured with integrity, character would be developed alongside academic excellence, and every child would be given the opportunity to build a brighter future.
